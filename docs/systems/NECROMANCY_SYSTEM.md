# Necromancy System

## Purpose

Necromancy is the central interaction language of the game, spanning investigation, healing, politics, crafting, combat, travel, companions, and endgame law.

## Action families

- perceive death residue;
- communicate with spirits/echoes;
- preserve body or identity;
- animate;
- bind;
- negotiate covenant;
- command;
- repair;
- transfer;
- divide or merge;
- release;
- destroy;
- create sanctuary;
- manipulate bone/flesh;
- write pacts.

## Resolution inputs

- actor skills and state;
- target Vessel/Name/Memory/Will/Thread;
- consent claim;
- relationship;
- materials and site;
- time;
- witnesses;
- opposing authority;
- world metaphysical state;
- deterministic seed.

## Method history

Each action emits method tags such as:

- consensual;
- coerced;
- deceptive;
- destructive;
- restorative;
- identity-preserving;
- body-desecrating;
- public;
- secret;
- emergency;
- promise-fulfilling;
- institutionally authorized.

Narrative systems respond to witnessed or credibly learned method history.

## Control capacity

The protagonist has bounded direct control. Capacity depends on skill, equipment, anchors, distance, complexity, resistance, captain hierarchy, and autonomy agreements.

Voluntary autonomous undead may require less direct capacity but can disagree.

## Failure

Failure types:

- no effect;
- incomplete information;
- body damage;
- spirit instability;
- lost control;
- mistaken identity;
- hostile attention;
- memory contamination;
- pact debt;
- corruption;
- permanent loss when clearly telegraphed.

## Corruption

Corruption is not a generic evil meter. It represents structural changes caused by power sources and repeated methods. Different corruption types unlock power and alter perception/body/relationships.

## Humanity

Humanity tracks connection to embodied mortal experience, empathy, vulnerability, and memory of Earth. Low Humanity does not automatically mean villainy; high Humanity does not excuse harm.

## Legality and claims

Bodies and spirits can have competing claims from the deceased, family, state, religion, battlefield authority, contract, and public safety. Mechanics record claims; story determines legitimacy disputes.

## Scaling

Progression stages:

1. perceive one dead person;
2. animate or release individuals;
3. maintain named companions;
4. delegate through captains;
5. establish sanctuary/law;
6. operate formations;
7. govern undead populations;
8. rewrite metaphysical covenant.

## Safeguards

- body does not imply identity;
- echo is not automatically non-person;
- consent has scope and revocation;
- history cannot be erased by respec;
- powerful actions have detectable consequences;
- no skill bypasses all metaphysical constraints.
