# Emergent Archetypes

## Purpose

The player does not select a moral class. The game recognizes patterns of behavior and unlocks identities, reactions, and opportunities based on what the player repeatedly does.

## Principles

- Archetypes emerge from behavior, not one dialogue choice.
- Multiple archetypes can coexist or conflict.
- They are descriptive, not a universal morality score.
- Hidden progress is communicated through narrative signals.
- Archetypes never invalidate an otherwise legal core build without warning.

## Candidate archetypes

- Grave Shepherd;
- Memory Thief;
- Bone Sovereign;
- Covenant Keeper;
- Covenant Breaker;
- Architect of Second Life;
- Merciful Oblivionist;
- King of Borrowed Names;
- Liberator of Vessels;
- Ashen Reformer;
- Hollow Citizen;
- Divine Usurper;
- Keeper of the Last Door.

## Evidence model

Archetype progress draws from:

- method history;
- promises;
- consent patterns;
- resource sacrifices;
- faction policies;
- treatment of named undead;
- memory actions;
- public legends;
- capstones;
- end-of-life decisions.

Each piece of evidence has weight, recency policy, visibility, and source task.

## Stages

- latent;
- recognized by close companions;
- self-recognized;
- publicly attributed;
- institutionally embodied;
- metaphysically established.

## Effects

Archetypes may:

- reveal skill transformations;
- unlock teachers or rivals;
- alter dialogue intent;
- change companion interpretation;
- create followers and enemies;
- qualify governance policies;
- affect ritual signatures;
- unlock covenant clauses;
- shape epilogues.

## Conflict

Examples:

- Covenant Keeper versus strategic breach;
- Grave Shepherd versus coercive army doctrine;
- Memory Thief versus identity-restoration work.

Conflict produces crisis content and hybrid variants rather than deleting prior history.

## Player agency

The player can accept, reject, reinterpret, or struggle against a recognized archetype. Rejection affects self-identity but cannot make witnesses forget behavior.

## UI

Do not show raw morality points. The Chronicle may display recognized patterns, evidence examples, and known consequences after sufficient discovery.

## Balance and testing

Simulations verify no archetype becomes mandatory for core completion, evidence is reachable through multiple builds, contradictory states resolve consistently, and old saves migrate safely.
