# Economy and Resource Design

## Purpose

Economy creates pressure among survival, power, dignity, governance, and promises. It must not encourage meaningless grinding.

## Resource domains

- coin;
- food and living supplies;
- ritual materials;
- repair materials;
- Essence;
- control capacity;
- bodies/remains with provenance;
- spirit anchors;
- knowledge;
- favors and debts;
- political legitimacy;
- time;
- territory labor and infrastructure.

## Principles

- no universal currency buys every form of power;
- important resources have multiple routes;
- acquisition method produces consequences;
- prices respond to region/state without chaotic randomness;
- no infinite arbitrage;
- no mandatory repetitive farming;
- civilian needs remain visible at strategic scale.

## Markets

Market types:

- ordinary trade;
- funerary services;
- black-market remains;
- memory exchange;
- imperial requisition;
- Hollow covenant exchange;
- faction favors;
- relic auction;
- territory procurement.

## Remains and souls

Mechanically trackable does not mean ethically fungible. Ownership claims, consent, identity, origin, law, and witness state affect use and trade.

## Rewards

Quest rewards may be:

- material;
- knowledge;
- relationship;
- title;
- access;
- law/policy;
- teacher;
- safe route;
- public narrative;
- future obligation.

Avoid rewards that invalidate the quest's moral consequence.

## Territory economy

Aggregate needs and production. Policies and infrastructure change rates. Reports show cause and uncertainty. The player responds to exceptional choices rather than daily accounting.

## Inflation and progression

Costs scale through new resource categories and institutional complexity, not by making bread cost a thousand coins. Early resources remain meaningful for people and local decisions.

## Difficulty

Scarcity settings change availability and recovery while preserving story routes. Accessibility never affects economic punishment.

## Simulation

Build reports for income/sinks, resource bottlenecks, dominant loops, quest reward distribution, territory survival, and no-grind completion paths.
