# Promises and Covenants

## Purpose

Words create persistent obligations. A promise can outlive a body, become political law, empower magic, destroy trust, or qualify a clause in the Last Covenant.

## Obligation types

- informal promise;
- witnessed oath;
- reciprocal agreement;
- service covenant;
- funerary instruction;
- political treaty;
- divine pact;
- command restriction;
- debt;
- final covenant clause.

## Promise record

- stable ID;
- parties;
- beneficiary;
- exact known wording;
- player-understood intent;
- scope;
- conditions;
- deadline or duration;
- witnesses;
- authority;
- consideration/payment;
- revocation/amendment rules;
- conflicts;
- fulfillment evidence;
- breach evidence;
- secrecy/publicity;
- enforcement;
- status.

The engine distinguishes wording from interpretation.

## Lifecycle

1. offer;
2. clarify or leave ambiguity;
3. accept/refuse;
4. record witnesses and authority;
5. create obligations;
6. detect opportunities/conflicts;
7. fulfill, amend, transfer, dispute, waive, or breach;
8. apply social and metaphysical consequences;
9. preserve historical outcome.

## Conflicts

Promises can become mutually incompatible. The UI warns about known direct conflicts but does not reveal secret facts. The player may:

- renegotiate;
- prioritize;
- find a loophole;
- transfer duty;
- compensate;
- accept breach;
- satisfy both through rare earned capability.

## Spirit and posthumous promises

A person's death does not automatically void an obligation. Validity depends on wording, law, identity continuity, and beneficiary. An undead successor may inherit, reject, or contest an oath.

## Enforcement levels

- conscience/reputation;
- relationship;
- legal;
- factional;
- ritual;
- Name-bound;
- divine;
- world-covenant.

Greater enforcement gives power and certainty but reduces flexibility.

## Example conflict

The player promises a family to release a soldier's spirit. Later, the soldier voluntarily requests temporary service to defend the family. Resolution depends on wording, consent, urgency, and whether amendment is possible.

## UI

The Covenant Ledger shows:

- active known promises;
- parties and witnesses;
- current interpretation;
- deadlines;
- known conflicts;
- fulfillment opportunities;
- risk of selected actions.

Hidden clauses appear only after discovery.

## Integration

Promises affect:

- companion trust and ideology;
- faction diplomacy;
- titles/legends;
- skill discovery;
- undead autonomy;
- political legitimacy;
- ritual strength;
- final covenant eligibility;
- epilogues.

## Authoring requirements

Every major promise defines fulfillment, breach, ambiguity, unavailable-party handling, identity-change handling, deadlines, and downstream references.

## Testing

Test duplicate fulfillment, identity transfer, death, contradictory promises, amendment, witness loss, secret discovery, deadline progression, save migration, and ending enforcement.
