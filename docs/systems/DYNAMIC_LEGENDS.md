# Dynamic Legends and Reputation Narratives

## Purpose

The world interprets the protagonist through stories. Different groups can describe the same act differently, and those descriptions create future gameplay.

## Distinction

- **Title:** formally or metaphysically recognized achievement.
- **Rumor:** transmitted claim with uncertain accuracy.
- **Legend:** durable narrative assembled from repeated acts and major events.
- **Public identity:** the Name and role a population associates with the protagonist.
- **Propaganda:** deliberately promoted interpretation.

## Legend record

- legend ID/family;
- subject identity;
- initiating events;
- method tags;
- witnesses/sources;
- regional variants;
- faction interpretation;
- credibility;
- emotional tone;
- spread;
- counters;
- active effects;
- historical persistence.

## Example interpretations

After raising defenders to save Dunmire:

- villagers: **Gravekeeper of Dunmire**;
- Church radicals: **Corpse-Thief of Saint Orra**;
- Hollow Court: **He Who Returned Our Names**;
- Empire: **Unlicensed Strategic Necromancer**;
- Shepherds: interpretation depends on consent and release.

## Effects

Legends influence:

- first impressions;
- intimidation and trust;
- followers/refugees;
- prices and access;
- enemy preparation;
- teacher interest;
- political legitimacy;
- assassination or recruitment attempts;
- available disguises;
- epilogue history.

## Spread

Legend spread depends on witnesses, travel routes, institutions, literacy, faction networks, contradiction, time, and deliberate messaging. It is deterministic and visible through regional reports.

## Contesting a legend

The player may:

- provide evidence;
- silence or persuade witnesses;
- create a public demonstration;
- adopt the title;
- reject it;
- redirect credit;
- expose propaganda;
- build an institution that embodies another narrative.

A legend cannot be erased merely by paying currency.

## Identity interaction

If the player changes vessel or Name, legends may attach to the body, title, public person, or suspected continuity. Mistaken identity creates authored conflicts.

## UX

Display what each known region/faction appears to believe, supporting evidence, and likely consequences. Do not reveal secret observers.

## Authoring

Major quests define candidate narrative interpretations based on outcome and method. Generic templates cover repeated behavior without producing nonsensical titles.

## Testing

Test contradictory legends, regional spread, identity change, witness removal, propaganda, thresholds, save migration, and epilogue composition.
