# Act V — The Last Covenant

## Purpose

Act V converts the player's entire campaign into a final constitutional and metaphysical act. It is not a single boss followed by an ending selection.

## World crisis

The old covenant fails. Effects depend on previous state:

- spirits cannot depart;
- divine domains collapse into mortal regions;
- the Below manifests through erased identities;
- conflicting rulers claim emergency authority;
- undead bindings weaken or become self-aware;
- birth and death cease to be reliable opposites.

## Final campaign structure

### V.1 — Gather the Named

Call allies, vassals, citizens, gods, enemies, and witnesses. Prior promises decide who answers.

### V.2 — The Cost Ledger

The game reveals accumulated costs: erased souls, preserved names, coerced service, deaths avoided, territories sustained, and debts unpaid.

### V.3 — War at the Last Door

Strategic confrontation shaped by faction outcomes. Combat, diplomacy, sacrifice, legal challenge, ritual, and infiltration routes can overlap.

### V.4 — The Assembly of Claims

Representatives argue what the new order must protect. The player cannot satisfy incompatible claims without rare earned compromises.

### V.5 — Write the Covenant

Construct final clauses from unlocked knowledge and institutional support.

### V.6 — Enforcement

A covenant requires an enforcement source: gods, distributed mortal institutions, the Hollow Court, an automated ritual network, the protagonist, the Below, or shared layered authority.

### V.7 — The Last Choice

Resolve protagonist body, Vael, companions, gods, and the right to final death.

## Covenant dimensions

- ownership of soul;
- consent standard;
- right to remain dead;
- right to return;
- memory preservation;
- status of copies and composites;
- divine sustenance;
- barrier maintenance;
- undead citizenship;
- body/remains rights;
- inheritance;
- enforcement;
- amendment procedure;
- emergency powers;
- protagonist exception.

## Eligibility

Clauses require evidence from play:

- Shepherd clauses from consensual practice and sanctuary;
- sovereignty clauses from functioning undead government;
- distributed maintenance from decentralized Ashen Engine outcome;
- divine reform from Church allies and Gate knowledge;
- individual immortality from advanced Soulcraft;
- species transformation from Fleshweaving;
- force-based enforcement from Grave Dominion/empire;
- contract enforcement from Pale Covenant.

The player cannot write unsupported ideals into reality.

## Final opposition

The final enemy varies:

- a Gatekeeper god;
- the Nameless Below;
- Vael;
- the player's former vessel;
- a coalition rejecting unilateral rule;
- the player's own command network;
- no single enemy, only an impossible negotiation.

## Final verification

Before resolving, the engine validates:

- clause compatibility;
- enforcement capacity;
- outstanding pacts;
- sacrificed anchors;
- faction acceptance;
- companion decisions;
- barrier viability;
- player identity continuity.

Invalid combinations produce authored crisis choices, not a technical error.

## Epilogue

Epilogues are assembled from authored modules based on canonical state. Modules cover immediate aftermath, each region, each companion, named undead, institutions, protagonist, and distant future. Contradictions are resolved through explicit precedence rules.
