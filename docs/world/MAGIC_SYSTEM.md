# Magic System

## Principle

Magic is the manipulation of relationships among matter, identity, memory, will, place, and covenant. Power always requires capacity, knowledge, access, cost, or obligation.

## Universal constraints

- Effects need a valid source and target.
- Greater distance, complexity, duration, and resistance increase cost.
- Information cannot be created from nothing.
- Permanent changes require anchors.
- Identity resists contradiction.
- Contracts bind according to wording, authority, and recognition.
- Repeated use leaves signatures.
- No spell grants unrestricted omnipotence.

## Necromancy

Manipulates Vessel, Name, Memory, Will, Thread, and death residue. Full disciplines are defined in skill documentation.

Costs may include:

- Essence;
- Focus;
- control capacity;
- bodily degradation;
- memory;
- corruption;
- ritual materials;
- consent or coercive resistance;
- pact debt;
- social discovery.

## Radiant magic

Uses divine contracts and Gate-aligned energy for healing, wards, revelation, and passage. It is effective against unstable undeath but not automatically morally pure.

Limitations:

- institutional authorization;
- doctrine-shaped effects;
- reduced reliability during Gate failure;
- risk of memory extraction;
- divine surveillance.

## Elemental and material arts

Manipulate physical properties through learned correspondences. Less central than necromancy but important for enemies, crafting, and environment.

## Name magic

Uses true recognition, testimony, records, and self-identification. Names cannot be reduced to secret passwords. Authority depends on the relationship between speaker, subject, and community.

## Covenant magic

Creates enforceable metaphysical obligations. Every covenant defines parties, consideration, scope, duration, enforcement, exceptions, and amendment.

## Memory magic

Reads, preserves, suppresses, shares, or reshapes memory. Memories are evidence, not infallible recordings.

## Alchemy and ritual craft

Stores repeatable magical relationships in materials, diagrams, timings, and tools. Enables characters without innate affinity to participate.

## Countermagic

Methods:

- break source;
- alter target relation;
- interrupt concentration;
- invalidate authority;
- satisfy or exploit contract;
- obscure Name;
- ground energy;
- free Will;
- destroy anchor;
- introduce contradictory memory.

## Rituals

Rituals are prepared multi-step commands. They require:

- known pattern;
- site;
- participants;
- components;
- timing;
- target;
- risk;
- interruption behavior;
- cleanup.

The engine validates preparation and resolves deterministic effects/checks.

## Magic visibility

Use produces sensory and investigable signatures. Different observers perceive based on skills, tools, and prior knowledge.

## Progression

Characters improve through knowledge, precision, capacity, new relationships, safer substitutions, and accepting greater consequences—not only larger numbers.

## Content authoring

Every magical effect specifies:

- affected metaphysical components;
- range/duration;
- cost;
- resistance;
- failure/partial outcomes;
- signature;
- counters;
- tags;
- canonical condition/effect operators.
